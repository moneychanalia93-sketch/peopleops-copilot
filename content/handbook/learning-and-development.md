# Learning and Development

## Learning budget
Every employee has a learning budget of $1,500 per year for courses, books, certifications and conferences. The budget resets each January and does not roll over.

## Conferences
Conference attendance needs your manager's approval before you register. Share three takeaways with your team after you return.

## Certifications
Certification exam fees are reimbursed after you pass. Keep the receipt and the pass confirmation.
