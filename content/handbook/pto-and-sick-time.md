# PTO, Holidays and Sick Time

## Accrual
Full-time employees accrue 1.67 days of paid time off (PTO) per month, which is 20 days per year. Accrual starts on your first day, and your balance is shown in the HR system.

## Requesting time off
Request PTO in the HR system at least two weeks ahead for any trip longer than five days. Your manager approves or declines the request within two business days. For one or two days off, a message to your manager is enough once the dates are on the shared team calendar.

## Company holidays
Lumen Harbor Labs observes 11 company holidays each year plus a winter shutdown between December 25 and January 1. Holidays differ by country and are listed on the shared holiday calendar.

## Sick time
Take sick time when you are unwell. Tell your manager by message before your normal start time and mark the day as Sick in the HR system. You do not need to share any details. A doctor's note is not required for absences of three days or fewer.

## Carryover
You can carry over up to five unused PTO days into the next calendar year. Days above five expire on December 31.
