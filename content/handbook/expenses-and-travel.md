# Expenses and Travel

## Submitting expenses
Submit expenses in the expense system within 30 days of the purchase. Attach a receipt for any expense over $25. Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle.

## Pre-approval
Get your manager's approval before spending more than $500 on a single item.

## Business travel
Book flights and hotels through the company travel tool. Fly economy for flights shorter than six hours. Meals while traveling are reimbursed up to $75 per day.

## Client entertainment
Client meals are allowed when there is a business purpose. Write the client name and purpose on the receipt.
