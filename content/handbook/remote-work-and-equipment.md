# Remote Work and Equipment

## Home office stipend
Every new employee receives a one-time home office stipend of $750 (or the local currency equivalent) to buy a desk, chair, monitor or similar items. Submit receipts through the expense system within 60 days of your start date.

## Laptops
Each employee gets a company laptop. Laptops are refreshed every three years. If your laptop is damaged, slow or not working, open an IT ticket and IT will repair or replace it.

## Internet and coworking
Remote employees can claim up to $50 per month for home internet. A coworking space is reimbursed up to $200 per month with your manager's approval.

## Working from another country
You may work from another country for up to 14 days per calendar year with your manager's approval. Longer stays need a review by the People team because of tax and payroll rules in the other country. Tell IT before you travel so security settings can be checked.
