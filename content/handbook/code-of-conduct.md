# Code of Conduct

## Gifts
You may accept a gift from a vendor or customer if it is worth less than $100 and is not tied to a decision. Tell your manager about any gift worth more than $100 and do not accept cash.

## Conflicts of interest
Tell your manager before you take outside work or join a board. You may not work for a competitor while employed here.

## Social media
You may talk about your work in public, but never share customer data, unreleased plans or internal numbers. Opinions you post are your own.

## Customer data
Customer data may only be stored in approved systems. Never copy it to personal email, personal drives or chat tools that are not approved.
