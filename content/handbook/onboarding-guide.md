# New Hire Onboarding Guide

## Before your first day
Your laptop ships three to five days before your start date. You will receive a welcome email with your day-one schedule and the name of your onboarding buddy.

## Your first day
On day one you set up your laptop, meet your manager, and join the 10:00 local-time orientation. Your onboarding buddy answers day-to-day questions. Your manager holds a one-to-one introduction.

## Your first week
Sign the handbook acknowledgment by day 3 and finish security awareness training by day 5. Meet your team and agree on your first-week goals with your manager. A check-in with your manager happens on day 7.

## Your first 30 days
Check-ins happen on day 1, day 7 and day 30. Complete benefits enrollment and compliance training before day 30. Write your 30-60-90 day goals with your manager.

## Who to ask
Ask your onboarding buddy for day-to-day questions, your manager for work priorities, and the People team in the people-team Slack channel for anything about paperwork or policy.
