# Payroll Basics

## Pay dates
Payday is twice a month, on the 15th and on the last business day of the month. If a payday falls on a weekend or holiday, you are paid on the business day before.

## Direct deposit
Update your direct deposit details in the HR system under Payment Details. Changes made by the 10th of the month take effect that month.

## Pay stubs and tax forms
Pay stubs are in the HR system. US employees receive their W-2 by January 31. UK and Canadian employees receive their year-end tax documents in the HR system.

## Updating personal details
Update your home address, emergency contact and name in the HR system under Personal Details. Address changes affect payroll tax, so update them as soon as you move.
