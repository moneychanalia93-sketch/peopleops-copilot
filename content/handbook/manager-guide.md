# Manager Guide

## Approving time off
Approve or decline PTO requests from your team within two business days, and check the team calendar so coverage is clear.

## Running one-to-ones
Hold a 30-minute one-to-one every week with each new hire and every two weeks with everyone else. Keep a shared agenda document.

## Onboarding a new hire
Assign an onboarding buddy, hold the day-1, day-7 and day-30 check-ins, and agree on first-week goals before the start date.

## Approving expenses
Approve expense reports from your team within three business days. Ask for a receipt when one is missing.

## Interview feedback
Submit your interview scorecard within 24 hours of each interview, before you read other interviewers' feedback.
