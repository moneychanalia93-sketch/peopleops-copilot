# Benefits Enrollment

## Enrollment window
New hires have 30 days from their start date to choose their benefits in the benefits portal. If you do not enroll in time, you must wait for the next annual open enrollment unless you have a qualifying life event.

## When coverage starts
Health insurance coverage begins on the first day of the month after your start date. Dental and vision follow the same date.

## Health insurance plans
US employees choose between three health plans: Basic, Plus and Premier. Employees in the UK and Canada are covered by local plans with similar tiers. The company pays 90 percent of the monthly premium for employees.

## Retirement savings
The company matches retirement contributions up to 4 percent of pay: a 401(k) match in the US, a pension match in the UK and an RRSP match in Canada. Matching begins after your first full pay period.

## Wellness and employee assistance
Employees receive a $50 monthly wellness stipend. The employee assistance program (EAP) is free, confidential and open 24 hours a day for all employees and their families.

## Life events
If you get married, have a child or lose other coverage, you have 30 days to update your enrollment. Contact the People team to start the change.
